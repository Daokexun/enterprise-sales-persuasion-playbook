# Enterprise Sales: 1-Page Consultative Selling & MEDDPPICC Cheatsheet

### 📐 The MEDDPPICC Deal Inspection Scorecard
| Letter | Component | Inspection Question | Green Flag Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M** | **Metrics** | What is the quantified business delta? | Explicit $ / hour / headcount targets signed off by client sponsor. |
| **E** | **Economic Buyer** | Have we met the P&L owner directly? | Direct conversation with VP/C-suite with budget authority. |
| **D** | **Decision Criteria** | How are options formally evaluated? | Technical, commercial, and operational criteria written in our favor. |
| **D** | **Decision Process** | What steps lead to procurement? | Explicit timeline from security review to board sign-off. |
| **P** | **Paper Process** | How does legal/procurement execute? | Master Services Agreement (MSA) and standard redline schedule confirmed. |
| **I** | **Identify Pain** | What is the quantified Cost of Inaction? | Technical pain linked to revenue loss or regulatory non-compliance. |
| **C** | **Champion** | Does the internal champion fight for us? | Champion tests competitors and coaches us on internal politics. |
| **C** | **Competition** | Who else is in the deal (including status quo)? | Competitor weaknesses mapped; in-house build cost quantified. |

---

### 🛡️ 3 High-Stakes Objection Re-anchors
- **In-House Tool**: *"Why spend 20% of senior engineering time maintaining plumbing when they could be shipping revenue features?"*
- **40% Cheaper Competitor**: *"License discounts are cheap; 4 hours of production downtime costs $1M. Let's compare 3-year TCO."*
- **Budget Freeze**: *"CFOs freeze expenses, but they fund self-amortizing projects. Let's look at immediate egress savings."*
