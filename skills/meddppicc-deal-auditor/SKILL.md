# MEDDPPICC Enterprise Deal Auditor

## 9-Vector Qualification Standard
Score each deal vector from 0 (Unknown) to 10 (Fully Validated):

1. **M (Metrics)**: Quantifiable economic impact (Top-line revenue acceleration vs Bottom-line cost reduction).
2. **E (Economic Buyer)**: Direct access to the P&L owner who can release budget when all else is frozen.
3. **D (Decision Criteria)**: Explicit technical, commercial, and legal criteria written in our favor.
4. **D (Decision Process)**: Step-by-step consensus, security review, executive sponsor sign-off.
5. **P (Partner)**: Cloud marketplace (AWS/Azure/GCP) or system integrator involvement.
6. **P (Paper Process)**: MSA redlines, infosec audit, vendor onboarding, and purchase order workflow.
7. **I (Identify Pain)**: The quantifiable Cost of Inaction (COI) if the client does nothing.
8. **C (Champion vs Coach)**: Champion has political power, skin in the game, and sells on our behalf when we leave the room.
9. **C (Competition)**: Status quo, internal DIY engineering build, or direct commercial rival.

## Risk Flags
- Score < 60: High slippage risk; do not forecast in commit pipeline.
- Score 60-80: Upside opportunity; requires Economic Buyer meeting and Champion testing.
- Score > 80: High-confidence commit; execute Paper Process reverse workback schedule.
